# Laboratory experiment sprites

Five standalone transparent PNG sprites for the IGNIS laboratory, generated from the laboratory scene as a visual reference.

- `experiment-chamber.png` — central containment chamber and plasma core
- `analysis-bench.png` — specimen-preparation bench with microscope and sample rack
- `control-console.png` — freestanding upright research control station
- `gas-cylinder-rack.png` — rack with four color-coded gas cylinders
- `scientist.png` — three-quarter-view technician holding a tablet

## Validation

All five assets passed specialist reviews for complete silhouettes, transparent cutouts without visible halos on a dark composited background, distinct equipment roles, stepped pixel clusters, and IGNIS palette/perspective. The PNGs were confirmed as RGBA with transparency. These are game-art assets; scientific labels and data are intentionally not represented.
